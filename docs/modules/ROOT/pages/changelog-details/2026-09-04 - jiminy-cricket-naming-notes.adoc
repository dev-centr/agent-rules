* TrademarkElite U.S. knockout for `jiminy cricket`: three Disney serials (**85298074**, **77098391**, **77979338**), all **DEAD — continued use not filed within grace period, un-revivable** (status dates 2021). Research note only — not clearance; other jurisdictions and copyright still matter.
